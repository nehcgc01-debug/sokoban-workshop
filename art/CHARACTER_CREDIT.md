# Character art

The player design began as Taisong Wang's hand-drawn concept: turquoise hair, blue fringe, orange flower, warm beige sweater, skirt, round mitten hands and rounded feet. The included `player.png` was generated with OpenAI image generation from that design at the user's request. It is AI-assisted prototype art, not solely hand-painted by the applicant. No third-party franchise character art was imported.

The gameplay renderer preserves the image aspect ratio. The original concept and this generated rendering are distinct work artifacts.

V4 revision removes the headband at the user’s request and retains the orange flower.

V5 uses the user-requested turquoise-to-blue gradient hair revision, without a headband. Mipmap filtering improves readability at gameplay scale.

V6 uses a closed-mouth front portrait and an AI-generated directional atlas derived from the same approved character design. Right, back and left poses are static images. Walking, leaning and sweat/exertion feedback are drawn procedurally in code, not hand-drawn frame-by-frame animations. The generated direction poses are approximations of the design, not a perfectly registered production animation rig.
